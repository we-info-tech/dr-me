Wrappers, Capitalism
& Real Innovation

Core idea

Innovation often comes from wrapping existing tools with better interfaces, coordination, and articulation — not from building from scratch.

Key concepts

  • Wrappers are products or tools built on top of existing ones.
  • Most people dismiss them, saying "it's just a wrapper" — but that is the innovation.
  • Ultimate wrapper: UI + coordination + business logic (e.g., Uber wrapped GPS + Maps + Payments).
  • Capitalism rewards articulation: building a better interface for tech and solving coordination problems.

Why people miss it

  • School teaches originality as the only virtue.
  • The real world rewards value creation over novelty.
  • Misunderstanding capitalism leads people to dismiss real entrepreneurship.

Skills to learn

  1. Product thinking — how people use tools.
  2. Systems thinking — how parts fit together.
  3. Coordination + ops
  4. UI/UX + storytelling

Actionable takeaway

Don't chase "from scratch" solutions. Instead, find what's broken, and wrap it better. That's how real impact is made.
