My Portfolio Building Blocks

I think of a portfolio as a set of building blocks, each with a different role. Some form a core, some add growth or diversification, and some keep money accessible.

These are the blocks I’m considering for my India allocation. The mix is personal; this is my framework, not a recommended allocation.

01 / India allocation

India
allocation.

01

Large-cap core

Nifty 50

A core allocation to established Indian large-cap companies, forming the foundation of the portfolio.

02

Emerging large-cap growth

Nifty Next 50

Exposure to the next group of companies beyond the Nifty 50, adding a growth-oriented block.

03

Growth potential

Midcap 150

Mid-sized companies can add growth potential, though their prices may fluctuate more than large-cap investments.

04

Momentum-driven growth

Smallcap 100 Momentum

A momentum-oriented allocation to smaller companies. It can bring higher volatility and sharper falls.

08

On my watchlist

Nifty Microcap 250

I’m watching this index and waiting for an ETF or mutual fund option that fits my approach.

09

Long-term sector conviction

Thematic & Sectoral ETFs

I use these selectively, focusing on industries I understand. For me, they’re long-term bets rather than short-term trades.

02 / Commodities

Commodities &
precious metals.

05

Diversification

Gold & Silver BeES

Precious metals can behave differently from equities and may diversify a portfolio, but their prices can fluctuate too.

03 / Real estate

Real
estate.

06A

Real estate exposure

REITs

Listed real estate investment trusts provide exposure to property assets without directly owning the properties.

06B

Infrastructure exposure

InvITs

Listed infrastructure investment trusts provide exposure to infrastructure assets without directly owning or operating them.

04 / Mutual funds

Mutual
funds.

07

Liquidity & stability

Money Market

I use a money market mutual fund to hold my emergency fund and dry powder. This keeps that money accessible, though the fund is not risk-free.

A note on risk

These blocks are not fixed allocation recommendations. Investments can overlap, fluctuate, and lose value. The right mix depends on personal goals, time horizon, responsibilities, and comfort with risk.
