How I Plan to Live
After 40, in Freedom

My ideal day after 40 is twelve focused hours split into three blocks: people, work, and health.

This is a plan, not a promise. It describes how I want to use the time that financial freedom is meant to give me.

Block Hours Focus
Morning 4 hrs Family and friends.
Afternoon 4 hrs Calls and deep work.
Evening 4 hrs Health, gym, martial arts, and family again.

Why this shape

  • Relationships get the freshest part of the day, not the leftovers.
  • Work is concentrated in one block, so calls and deep work get full attention.
  • Health is a daily commitment, and the day ends with family.

Why after 40

This schedule only works once my finances no longer depend on trading every hour for income. The plan that gets me there is explained in Retirement by Design.
